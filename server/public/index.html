Import from history

Google asks to let Signal Shelf see your YouTube account. It only reads your likes, once.

On a computer, the Chrome extension can Pick from YouTube history.

Use your full watch history file
  1. Open Google Takeout for YouTube.
  2. Under All YouTube data included, keep only history. Create the export.
  3. When Google emails it, unzip and choose watch-history here.

The file stays on your device.

    Use your Mac's Podcasts library
    1. On your Mac, quit the Podcasts app.
    2. Open Finder, press Shift ⌘ G and paste this:
      ~/Library/Group Containers/243LU875E5.groups.com.apple.podcasts/Documents
    3. Choose MTLibrary.sqlite here.

    The file stays on your device.
